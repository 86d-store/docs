## Can I run a real store on this?

Not yet, and not as a general claim about the product. Every capability earns [Stable](/resources/glossary#capability-maturity) on its own, through its contract, its failure behavior, its tests, its documentation, and production evidence. None has.

See [Public Beta status](/resources/public-beta) and [Versioning and maturity](/resources/versioning).

## What is the difference between 86d.store and 86d.app?

**86d.store** is the open-source [Store Runtime](/concepts/architecture): a [Storefront](/concepts/storefront), a [Store Admin](/concepts/admin), your Features and Integrations, and one Store's commerce data. You run it.

**86d.app** is the optional managed product that runs it for you, plus billing, provider onboarding, and agent access. [86d Console](/resources/glossary#86d-console) is the interface you sign in to. The [Control Plane](/concepts/architecture) is the authority behind it.

See [What is 86d?](/introduction).

## What is the license?

86d.store, including all 101 first-party [Modules](/concepts/modules), is [MIT-licensed](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/blob/main/LICENSE). Use it, change it, host it, sell what it sells, fork it. 86d.app is a separate proprietary service you can ignore entirely.

## What does it cost?

Self-hosting costs you nothing in license fees. You pay for your own server, database, and providers, the same as any software you run.

[86d Cloud](/resources/cloud-plans) is the managed option: a planned 30-day trial with $5 credit, then Launch at $1 per Active Store per month for twelve months, or Premium at $20 per Active Store per month, plus metered infrastructure and services. Enterprise is negotiated. That offer is not activatable yet.

## Can I run it without an 86d account?

Yes. A standalone Store Runtime reads local configuration, uses infrastructure and credentials you control, and never calls 86d. No [86d Account](/resources/glossary#86d-account) is involved.

## Where can I host it?

Anywhere that runs a Next.js application and PostgreSQL. These docs cover Docker Compose, Railway, and Vercel with Neon.

See [Deployment and hosting](/deployment).

## Can two Stores share a deployment or a database?

No, and this is a hard boundary rather than a recommendation. One Store Runtime, one commerce database, per Store. That is what keeps your data portable and one Store's problem from becoming another's.

See [Store isolation](/concepts/architecture#one-store-one-database).

## Feature, Integration, Module, Connection: what is the difference?

- A **[Feature](/resources/glossary#feature)** is something your Store does.
- An **[Integration](/concepts/connections)** is something your Store does with an outside company.
- A **[Module](/concepts/modules)** is the package that delivers either one.
- A **[Connection](/concepts/connections)** is the specific provider account an Integration is configured to use.

## Does everything in the catalog work?

No. The catalog is a list of packages, not a list of promises. Every entry is currently Experimental with no recorded evidence. Read the Module's page, its source, its tests, and how it behaves when a provider is down before you enable it.

See the [Module catalog](/modules/overview).

## Can I use something other than PostgreSQL?

Not with a supported adapter today. The Store Runtime uses Drizzle and PostgreSQL.

## How do payments work right now?

You bring your own Stripe, PayPal, Square, or Braintree account, and the [Module](/concepts/modules) for it connects that account to your Store. 86d charges you nothing per Checkout on this model, and it is sandbox-only for now.

[86d Payments](/resources/glossary#86d-payments), where 86d handles onboarding and settlement at one all-in rate, is a separate thing that does not exist yet.

See [Set up a payment provider](/guides/payment-integrations).

## Can an agent run my Store?

Not yet. The [Docs MCP server](/resources/mcp) is read-only: it searches and reads these pages and cannot see or change a Store. Authorized product operations through Remote OAuth MCP are still being built.

See [How agents work with 86d](/concepts/agentic-design).

## How do I update?

Read the release and migration notes first, then update dependencies, run migrations, regenerate, and run the health checks before you deploy anything.

See [Versioning and maturity](/resources/versioning) and the [changelog](/resources/changelog).

## Where do I report something broken?

Ordinary bugs go to [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/issues). Anything security-relevant goes privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/security/advisories), not a public issue.
