List on Wish from your existing catalog. The Wish [Module](/concepts/modules) is meant to publish [Products](/modules/products), keep stock in step, and bring sales back into your [Orders](/modules/orders).

Nothing here has been verified: listings, [Inventory](/modules/inventory), Orders, refunds, [Fulfillment](/modules/fulfillment), and provider events are all unproven. Read the source before you evaluate it.

**Source:** [`modules/wish`](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/tree/main/modules/wish) · **npm:** [`@86d-app/wish`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@86d-app/wish)

## Installation

```sh
86d module add wish
```
