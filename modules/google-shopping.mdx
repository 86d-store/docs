</Warning>

The Google Shopping [Module](/concepts/modules) generates a product feed in the format Google Merchant Center expects and, when configured, pushes it to Merchant Center through the Content API. Conversion tracking uses your `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_TAG_MANAGER_ID` for return on ad spend (ROAS) reporting.

**Source:** [`modules/google-shopping`](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/tree/main/modules/google-shopping) · **npm:** [`@86d-app/google-shopping`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@86d-app/google-shopping)

## Installation

```sh
86d module add google-shopping
```

## Store endpoints

| Method | Path | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/feeds/google-shopping.xml` | Product feed for Merchant Center |
