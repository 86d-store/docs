Put your Products in front of people on X. The X Shop [Module](/concepts/modules) is meant to publish a product surface and link it back to your [Storefront](/concepts/storefront).

Nothing here has been verified: publishing, the provider approval process, product links, and failure handling are all unproven. Read the source before you evaluate it.

## Installation

```sh
86d module add x-shop
```
