Get your Products into Pinterest's shopping surfaces. The Pinterest Shop [Module](/concepts/modules) is meant to generate a product feed Pinterest can ingest and keep it up to date.

Nothing here has been verified: feed generation, provider ingestion, how often updates land, and what happens when a feed fails are all unproven. Read the source before you evaluate it.

**Source:** [`modules/pinterest-shop`](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/tree/main/modules/pinterest-shop) · **npm:** [`@86d-app/pinterest-shop`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@86d-app/pinterest-shop)

## Installation

```sh
86d module add pinterest-shop
```
