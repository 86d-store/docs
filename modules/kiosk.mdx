A self-serve terminal in your shop, running your own Store. The Kiosk [Module](/concepts/modules) is meant to turn a tablet into a locked-down ordering screen that resets between customers.

Nothing here has been verified: device lockdown, idle reset, [Checkout](/modules/checkout), [Payment](/modules/payments), and point-of-sale behavior are all unproven. A kiosk sits in public, unattended, next to a payment terminal, so treat unverified here as meaning genuinely unverified. Read the source before you evaluate it.

**Source:** [`modules/kiosk`](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/tree/main/modules/kiosk) · **npm:** [`@86d-app/kiosk`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@86d-app/kiosk)

## Installation

```sh
86d module add kiosk
```
