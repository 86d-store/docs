The Vendors [Module](/concepts/modules) turns one [Store](/resources/glossary#store) into a multi-vendor marketplace. Each product belongs to one vendor, and each [Order](/concepts/commerce-model) splits into per-vendor sub-orders for fulfillment. You take a configurable commission and pay vendors out on a schedule.

**Source:** [`modules/vendors`](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/tree/main/modules/vendors) · **npm:** [`@86d-app/vendors`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@86d-app/vendors)

## Installation

```sh
86d module add vendors
```
