On-demand local delivery through Favor. The Favor [Module](/concepts/modules) is meant to quote a delivery at [Checkout](/modules/checkout), dispatch a courier when the [Order](/modules/orders) is placed, and track it to the door.

Nothing here has been verified: quoting, dispatch, cancellation, tracking, price adjustments, and provider events are all unproven. Read the source before you evaluate it.

**Source:** [`modules/favor`](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/tree/main/modules/favor) · **npm:** [`@86d-app/favor`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@86d-app/favor)

## Installation

```sh
86d module add favor
```
