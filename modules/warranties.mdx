</Warning>

The Warranties [Module](/concepts/modules) lets you sell warranty plans alongside products at [Checkout](/concepts/commerce-model): extended coverage, accidental damage, or theft. A claim then moves through `submitted -> reviewing -> approved -> resolved`, with `rejected` as the terminal alternative.

**Source:** [`modules/warranties`](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/tree/main/modules/warranties) · **npm:** [`@86d-app/warranties`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@86d-app/warranties)

## Installation

```sh
86d module add warranties
```
