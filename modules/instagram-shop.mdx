Tag your Products in Instagram posts and let people buy them. The Instagram Shop [Module](/concepts/modules) is meant to publish your catalog to an Instagram commerce account and keep it current.

Nothing here has been verified: catalog publishing, the provider approval process, [Inventory](/modules/inventory) sync, [Orders](/modules/orders), and provider events are all unproven. Read the source before you evaluate it.

**Source:** [`modules/instagram-shop`](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/tree/main/modules/instagram-shop) · **npm:** [`@86d-app/instagram-shop`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@86d-app/instagram-shop)

## Installation

```sh
86d module add instagram-shop
```
