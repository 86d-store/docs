Let customers take a photo in your shop and share it. The Photo Booth [Module](/concepts/modules) is meant to capture an image, store it, and let the person share or download it.

Nothing here has been verified: consent capture, media storage, sharing, notifications, and kiosk behavior are all unproven. This Module handles photographs of real people, so the consent and retention gaps matter more than usual. Read the source before you evaluate it.

**Source:** [`modules/photo-booth`](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/tree/main/modules/photo-booth) · **npm:** [`@86d-app/photo-booth`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@86d-app/photo-booth)

## Installation

```sh
86d module add photo-booth
```
