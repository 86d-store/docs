The Social Proof [Module](/concepts/modules) shows [Shoppers](/resources/glossary#shopper) evidence that other people are buying: live purchase notifications, recent-order counts, viewer counts on product pages, and trust badges you configure.

**Source:** [`modules/social-proof`](https://github.com/86d-app/86d/tree/main/modules/social-proof) · **npm:** [`@86d-app/social-proof`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@86d-app/social-proof)

## Installation

```sh
86d module add social-proof
```

## Components

Use these components in your MDX [Template](/concepts/templates) files.

- `<RecentPurchases />`
- `<ViewerCount productId={id} />`
- `<TrustBadges />`
